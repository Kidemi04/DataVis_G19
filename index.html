Data notes

The Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics published these hospitalisation data for 2011–2021.

Q5 uses hospitalisations with a known area of residence. Each share uses the total for the same population group and year. These shares describe where hospitalised people lived. Estimating rates of injury would require population data.
